About The Project

Understanding Crime Through Data Science

Our Crime Pattern Prediction & Mapping system leverages machine learning algorithms to analyze historical crime data from India, identify patterns, predict future trends, and visualize crime hotspots on interactive maps.

Data-Driven

Upload any crime dataset (CSV) and our system auto-detects columns, crime types, states, and time periods for seamless analysis.

Ensemble ML Models

Uses Random Forest & Gradient Boosting ensemble for robust predictions with cross-validation and performance metrics.

India-Focused

Specifically designed for Indian crime datasets with built-in state coordinates, IPC categorization, and regional analysis.

Report Generation

Generate comprehensive PDF reports with charts, risk assessments, predictions, and actionable recommendations.
